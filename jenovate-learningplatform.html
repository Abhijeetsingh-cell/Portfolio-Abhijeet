01

The problem

Students often work hard in school but still struggle to connect what they're learning to real career confidence. Course content and certificates alone don't close that gap.

Jenovate was built around practical learning, guided mentorship, and projects that let learners prove what they can actually do — not just what they've studied.

Goal: give students structured, mentor-guided paths that build portfolio-ready skills, not just completion certificates.

02

What the platform offers

  • Learning paths Structured courses built around real skills, projects, and mentor guidance rather than passive content delivery.
  • Mentorship Direct access to experienced industry professionals who share practical, career-ready insight.
  • Projects Industry-focused project work that strengthens a learner's portfolio and shows recruiters real, applied skill.
  • Simulations Interactive workplace scenarios that build practical experience before students step into a real job.
03

My contribution

I worked on the frontend experience of the Jenovate learning platform, focusing on a clean interface that makes learning resources and platform content easy to explore.

I also worked with JavaScript and REST API integration to make the platform more interactive and connect the frontend with dynamic data.

What's next

Future improvements could include richer learner dashboards, progress tracking, personalized learning paths, and stronger project-based learning flows.

The next focus is making the platform more scalable and improving the overall experience for students as they move from learning to building portfolio-ready projects.
